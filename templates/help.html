How to Use EyeVerse

Step-by-step guide to analyzing retinal images

User Guide

Step 1: Prepare Your Image

Make sure you have a retinal fundus image ready:

  • Image format: JPG or PNG
  • File size: Maximum 16MB
  • Image should be clear and well-lit
  • Retina should be centered in the image

Step 2: Upload Image

You can upload your image in two ways:

  • Click to Browse: Click on the upload area and select your image file
  • Drag and Drop: Drag your image file directly into the upload area

Step 3: Analyze

After uploading, you'll see a preview of your image:

  • Review the image preview
  • Click the "Analyze Image" button
  • Wait a few seconds for AI analysis (usually < 5 seconds)

Step 4: View Results

The results page will show:

  • Diagnosis: DR severity level (No DR, Mild, Moderate, Severe, or Proliferative)
  • Confidence: How confident the AI is in its prediction
  • Probabilities: Likelihood for each class
  • Recommendations: Clinical actions based on the diagnosis

Step 5: Download Report

You can download a detailed text report containing:

  • Complete diagnosis and findings
  • All probability scores
  • Clinical recommendations
  • Timestamp of analysis

Click the "Download Report" button to save the report as a text file.

Understanding Results

Class 0: No DR

No signs of diabetic retinopathy detected. Retina appears healthy. Regular annual screening recommended.

Class 1: Mild DR

Early stage with few microaneurysms. Requires monitoring every 6-12 months.

Class 2: Moderate DR

More pronounced symptoms with multiple lesions. Monitoring every 3-6 months. Consider specialist referral.

Class 3: Severe DR

Advanced stage with extensive damage. IMMEDIATE specialist consultation required. High risk of vision loss.

Class 4: Proliferative DR

Most severe stage with new blood vessel formation. EMERGENCY medical attention needed. Risk of blindness without immediate treatment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How accurate is the system?

The system achieves 78-85% accuracy on test data. However, it should be used as a screening tool only. Final diagnosis must be confirmed by a qualified ophthalmologist.

Q: What image formats are supported?

JPG and PNG formats are supported. Maximum file size is 16MB.

Q: Is my data stored?

Uploaded images are temporarily stored during analysis and can be deleted after use. No personal health information is collected.

Q: Can I use this for medical diagnosis?

NO. This system is for educational purposes only. It is NOT a replacement for professional medical diagnosis. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider.

Q: What should I do if I get a high-risk result?

If the system indicates Severe or Proliferative DR, seek immediate consultation with an ophthalmologist. Do not delay medical care based on these results.
